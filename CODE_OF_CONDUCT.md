# Code of Conduct

Spoon is built by people with different backgrounds, experience levels and opinions. We want
taking part to be a good experience for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and in code review.
- Critique ideas and code, not people. Explain the reasoning behind requested changes.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming.
- Accept decisions gracefully once they have been discussed, and credit others' work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or discriminatory remarks of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, chat — and to public
spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainers privately. **Maintainers: a conduct contact address still needs to
be set up and added here before the project is published.** Reports will be handled confidentially.

Maintainers may respond with a private warning, a public reminder, temporary restrictions, or a
permanent ban, depending on severity and history. People affected by a decision may ask another
maintainer to review it.
